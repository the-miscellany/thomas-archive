The card confirms what his obituary says: when Capt. Hiram Brown resigned, Samuel, then first lieutenant, was made captain in his place ("vice H. Brown").

## Transcription

> THOMAS, SAMUEL BROWN\
> Texas\
> Capt., Co. F, 13th Texas (Dismtd.) Cav. Reg.,\
> from Aug. 27, 1863; vice H. Brown, resigned;
