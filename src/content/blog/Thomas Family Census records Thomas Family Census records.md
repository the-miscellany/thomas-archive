In the course of the research I've done, I've been fortunate to locate a collection of census records dating from the 1790 census to the 1900 census on the Thomas family.

They are, in order:
![Theophilus Thomas census record.](/census/1790-census-theophilus.jpg)
Theophilus Thomas, Edgecombe, North Carolina, 1790 Census, 4th from the bottom, left column

![Theophilus Thomas census record.](/census/1800-census.jpg)
Theophilus Thomas, Edgecombe, North Carolina, 1800, Middle of the page (as Thomas, Theophilus)

![Benjamin Thomas census record.](/census/1810-benjamin-thomas.jpg)
Benjamin Thomas - Edgecombe, North Carolina, 1810, Second from bottom

![Benjamin Thomas census record.](/census/1816-benjamin-thomas-alabama.jpg)
Benjamin Thomas, Madison County, Alabama, 1816, record (no document)

![Shadrach Thomas census record.](/census/1850-census-shadrach-thomas.jpg)
Shadrach Thomas, San Augustine, Texas, 1850, Middle of the page

![Samuel Brown Thomas census record.](/census/1852-sbthomas.jpg)
Samuel Brown Thomas, Mariposa, California, 1852, Only Last name visible, 4 from bottom

![Shadrach Thomas census record.](/census/1860-sbthomas.jpg)
Shadrach Thomas, San Augustine, Texas, 1860, Number 15

![Samuel Brown Thomas census record.](/census/1860-sd-thomas.jpg)
Samuel Brown Thomas, Mariposa, California, 1860, Number 9

![Samuel Brown Thomas census record.](/census/1880-census-samuel-brown-thomas.jpg)
Samuel Brown Thomas, San Augustine, Texas, 1880, Number 43

![Samuel Brown Thomas census record.](/census/1900-sb-thomas.jpg)
Samuel Brown Thomas and William Clayborn Thomas, Nacogdoches, Texas, 1900, Number 63 and 69
